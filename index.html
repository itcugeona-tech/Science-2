Intellectual Revolution

The Copernican Revolution

The transformation from an Earth-centered view of the universe to a Sun-centered model of the planetary system.

BEFORE COPERNICUS

Geocentric Model

The traditional astronomical model placed Earth at the center.

The Sun, Moon, planets, and stars were understood to move around Earth.

This view was strongly associated with the astronomical system of Ptolemy.

🌍

COPERNICAN MODEL

Heliocentric Model

Copernicus proposed a model in which the Sun occupies the central position in the planetary system.

Earth and the other planets move around the Sun.

This challenged the established geocentric understanding of the cosmos.

☀

Sun at the center

Why It Mattered

It changed how people understood Earth's position in the cosmos and contributed to the development of modern astronomy.

Major Work

De revolutionibus orbium coelestium was published in 1543.
